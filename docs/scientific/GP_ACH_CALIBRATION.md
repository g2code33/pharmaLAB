# Calibration decision

**No approved calibration set.** Rodriguez used 10⁻⁹–10⁻⁴ M ACh, but did not publish a tabulated normal concentration series. Fig 3 varies ischemia duration and stimulus, not ACh concentration. Digitising those bars as dose–response points would change the independent variable. Neither maximum identifies both potency and slope. Suzuki measured shortening under load, not isometric force. Do not digitise unlabelled series in parsed PDF tables as if they were raw data.

Acquire independently licensed, preparation-matched normal biological replicates with baseline, mid-range and plateau observations. Record pretreatment, individual animal/preparation identifiers, wash and contact windows. First confirm equivalence of the preparation, solution, load and measurement. For traces, establish time/force axis scales, recorder speed transitions and event markers. Record calibration coordinates, repeated digitiser readings and separate digitisation error from published biological variability; archive a rights decision before redistributing points.

The exploratory `estimateCurve` interface requires at least three distinct preparation curves, known baseline, repeated plateau measurements and three distinct mid-range concentrations bracketing half response per curve. Emax is the mean plateau increment; linear least squares on logit(fraction) vs ln(M) yields Hill slope and EC50. No starting guesses; positive slope required. Results retain input IDs, constraints, per-preparation force residuals and RMSE. This transform weights errors differently from nonlinear fitting; it is **not** a replication of Suzuki's regression or an approved unbiased estimator. Across independent preparations an exact sign-test median interval is available when coverage can reach 95%; too few replicates returns null. Do not fit summaries or pooled points as independent preparations.

Future fits require protocol-specific review of heteroscedasticity, replicate design and parameter identifiability, then a new version and frozen calibration manifest. None of the fit results from software test fixtures enter the scientific registry.
