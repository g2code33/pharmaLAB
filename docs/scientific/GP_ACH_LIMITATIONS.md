2. Static slope, biological baseline, sampling of true tension and time-dependent coefficients remain uncalibrated. 3. The kinetics observations span short contacts, prolonged exposure, different loads, temperatures, solutions and recorder modalities. Parsed figures do not provide a sufficiently reliable axis/trace digitisation with extraction error. A secondary increase in tonus is not a single decay constant.

The supplied dose ratio 427, pA2 9.3, 200 nM and n=2 remain **unverified contextual leads**, not data records. Obtain and review those pages before using them. No antagonist implemented.
5. Dataset/figure redistribution rights are not cleared. No study PDFs, traces, images or reconstructed curves are bundled. The only plotted response endpoints are attributed text-summary facts.
6. A new run is an independently configured bath calculation, not a claim that all biochemical conditions reproduce any one paper. New parameter versions must keep the v1 reader and fixtures. Historical foundation notebooks have no ACh payload; opening them does not create one.
